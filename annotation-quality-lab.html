Annotation Quality Lab

Rate AI response quality on a 1–3 scale as two different labelers, then check whether the ratings can be trusted — agreement, chance-corrected agreement (Cohen's κ), accuracy against a gold set, and a disagreement queue.

Rubric — read before labeling

1 · Poor — Wrong, misleading, ignores the request, or leaves the user worse off. Factual errors default here.

2 · Adequate — Correct and on-topic, but thin, partial, or awkward. Usable, not impressive.

3 · Excellent — Accurate, complete, clear, well-judged. Nothing important missing.

When unsure between two ratings, pick the lower one and leave a note. Items marked GOLD carry a pre-set reference answer used to check accuracy, not just agreement.
